# DockColor

Semantic color roles used throughout [DockTheme](<dock-theme.md>) to style tabs, split bars, floating window chrome, and drop preview zones.

## Overview

`DockColor` defines semantic color constants used throughout `DockTheme`.

Encompasses 44 distinct roles covering:
- **Tab Header**: Active, inactive, hovered, pinned, and close button colors.
- **Split Handles**: Normal, hover, and dragging bar accents.
- **Drop Previews**: Fill, stroke, and center cross indicators for dock targets.
- **Floating Chrome**: Window title bar, borders, shadows, and resize grips.
